What you are watching

The cart moves to raise all three links from their downward equilibrium. A newly optimised six-second reference is tracked by time-varying feedback, then an upright LQR maintains balance.

The rods have uniformly distributed mass and rotational inertia. The endpoint masses are retained. Only the cart is actuated.

Verified physical replay

The trajectory is integrated from the nonlinear equations with force saturation. A second run halves the integration step. This is one tested nominal manoeuvre, not guaranteed recovery from arbitrary states.

Model, calculations and display

Cart: 0.8 kg. Three rods: 0.010 kg and 1/6 m each. Three endpoint masses: 0.2/3 kg each. Rod inertia about its centre: I = mL²/12. Cart viscous friction: 0.08 N·s/m. Free, frictionless hinges; rigid rods; ideal full-state measurements. Link collisions and contact with the cart or rail are not modelled.

The planner uses Hermite–Simpson collocation. The old trajectory is only an initial guess; the reference is recomputed with the massive-rod equations. Feasibility is assessed by independent forward integration, not by the appearance of the animation. No global or time-optimal solution is claimed.

Playback interpolates generalised coordinates using their velocities, then reconstructs fixed-length rods. Trails, colours and force-arrow scale are visual aids. The animation does not apply extra forces or correct positions. This stage has no mouse dragging.
