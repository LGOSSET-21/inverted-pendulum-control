How the handoff works

No state reset is used. The swing-up starts only after all three links remain within 0.5° of the downward equilibrium, angular speeds remain below 0.02 rad/s, and the cart is almost centred for 0.8 s.

No angle is imposed or clamped. The hand force acts through the nonlinear equations, with a smooth onset and a 0.4 N maximum. The controller can recover small disturbances, but cannot prevent every fall. Automatic recovery begins after release.

Educational numerical model: three uniform rods of 10 g each plus endpoint masses, passive frictionless joints, ideal sensing and actuation; collisions and rail end stops are not modelled. Recovery is not guaranteed for every disturbance. Model, interaction and verification.
