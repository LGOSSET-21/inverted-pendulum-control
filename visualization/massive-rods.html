The calculations behind the change

Each rod contributes translational kinetic energy of its centre of mass, rotational kinetic energy, and gravitational potential energy:

Trod = ½m|vcentre|² + ½Iω²   ·   I = mL²/12   ·   Vrod = mg ycentre

The coupled equations retain the form M(q)q̈ = f(q,q̇,u). Both the mass matrix and gravity terms change. The linearisation and Riccati equation are recomputed using the same Q and R, so controller gains change with the model.

The point masses remain 0.2/3 kg each; rod lengths remain 1/6 m; cart mass is 0.8 kg. Cart viscous friction is 0.08 N·s/m; hinges remain frictionless. Rods are rigid, with uniformly distributed mass. Sensing and force actuation remain ideal. This comparison changes total mass as well as mass distribution.

What has been verified?

Zero rod mass recovers the original equations. Independently assembled rigid-body energies match the matrix model; mechanical power balance and finite-difference linearisation are checked. All six trials are repeated with the integration step halved. Numerical checks and gains.
